Oliver Hyman-Metzger

I study American politics, with a focus on political behavior, judicial politics, and the institutions that shape executive power. My work uses natural experiments and other causal-inference designs to understand how political attitudes form and change.

Before Harvard, I spent three years at the Federal Reserve Board of Governors, where I worked on emergency lending programs and designed a crisis monitoring tool using real-time payments data. My research at the Board concerned whether monetary policy reinforces structural inequalities in access to credit. I graduated from Columbia University in 2023 with a B.A. in Economics–Political Science.
